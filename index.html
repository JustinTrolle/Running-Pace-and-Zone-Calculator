Regression Rate Test (Short / Long Distance)

What is the Regression Rate? The regression rate measures the percentage by which your per-mile pace slows when you double the race distance. A rate of ~6.5% is considered natural. A higher percentage indicates a need for more base, strength, and strength endurance work to build fatigue resistance. A lower percentage suggests a need for more speed, speed endurance, and potentially biomechanical work.

Default test: 800m / 1600m. You may use any two distances where one is exactly half the other.

Predicted Race Paces & Times

Predictions use your 5K time and regression rate to estimate paces at various distances. The formula: Time = 5K_Time × (Distance_Ratio) ^ (1 + Regression_Rate). A higher regression rate means more pace degradation over distance.

7-Zone Run Training Intensity Guide

How zones are calculated: Your LT2 (Lactate Threshold 2) pace is estimated as the pace you could sustain for a continuous effort — 35 min for Age-Group, 45 min for Performance, or 60 min for Elite athletes. LT1 is then set as a percentage of LT2 speed. Zones are distributed across these thresholds.
